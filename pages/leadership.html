The People Behind HER

HER Initiative is powered by people who believe that better health begins with better understanding.

Hannah Arkum Adiss
Founder & Executive Director

"One vision. Many voices. One movement."

Hannah Arkum Adiss is the founder of the Hormonal Empowerment & Restoration Initiative (HER Initiative), a Ghanaian organization dedicated to advancing hormonal-health awareness through education, empowerment, advocacy, and community engagement.

Through HER Initiative, she is working to create a platform where hormonal health can be discussed openly, understood more clearly, and recognized as an important part of overall health and well-being.
