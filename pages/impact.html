Our Outcomes

For us, impact is about more than how many people we reach. It is about what people learn. What conversations begin. What misconceptions are challenged. What confidence is gained. And what changes because someone now understands more than they did before.

We will track:

— People reached — the individuals who engage with HER through our educational and awareness activities.
— People educated — participants in structured educational programs and engagements.
— Communities engaged — reached through outreach, partnerships, and programs.
— Volunteers and advocates — people contributing time, skills, and voice to the movement.
— Partnerships — organizations and professionals working alongside us.
— Learning and outcomes — what participants learn, understand, and apply as a result of our work.

These numbers reflect where HER is today. As programmes scale, we will update this page with verified results — not projections.
