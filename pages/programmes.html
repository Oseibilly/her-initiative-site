Our Work

Hormonal health is not a conversation that belongs to one gender, one age group, or one community. HER Initiative takes an inclusive, life-course approach through four areas of work.

HER Women

Her hormones. Her health. Her voice.

We promote greater awareness and understanding of:

  • Menstrual health
  • Reproductive health education
  • PCOS awareness
  • Hormonal well-being
  • Body literacy
  • Health-seeking awareness

You deserve information you can understand.

Explore HER Women

HER Men

Because hormonal health belongs in every conversation.

HER Men creates opportunities for:

  • Greater awareness of male hormonal health
  • Education on hormonal changes throughout life
  • Open discussion and well-being support

Understanding your health is not a weakness. It is empowerment.

Explore HER Men

HER Youth

Knowledge today. Healthier futures tomorrow.

HER Youth provides educational opportunities on:

  • Puberty and hormonal changes
  • Menstrual health
  • Body awareness and well-being
  • When to seek appropriate support
Learn→ Ask→ Understand→ Act

  • Families
