HER Initiative

Your hormones are telling a story. You deserve to understand it.

Hormonal health is an essential part of our overall health and well-being — yet for many people, it remains poorly understood. Questions go unanswered, misinformation spreads, and important conversations are often avoided. HER Initiative is changing that.

Hormonal health is health.

And everyone deserves the opportunity to understand it.

We are building a movement that makes hormonal health easier to understand, easier to discuss, and harder to ignore.

What HER Stands For

HER is more than an acronym.

It represents a commitment to creating greater understanding, confidence, and support around hormonal health.

H

HORMONAL

We promote greater understanding of the role hormones play in health, development, well-being, and everyday life.

E

EMPOWERMENT

We equip individuals and communities with knowledge and confidence to ask informed questions, seek appropriate support, make informed decisions, and advocate for their health.

R

RESTORATION

We support pathways toward greater awareness, well-being, connection, and healthier communities.

HER is more than an acronym. It is what we are building.

Our Approach

From knowledge to action.

We believe that meaningful change begins with understanding. Our work moves through four connected stages.

01

Educate

We make credible hormonal-health information accessible, understandable, and relevant.

02

Empower

We help individuals and communities develop the knowledge and confidence to participate more actively in decisions about their health.

03

Advocate

We raise awareness of hormonal health and encourage stronger conversations across communities and institutions.

04

Connect

We bring individuals, communities, professionals, organizations, and partners together around a shared commitment to better hormonal-health awareness.
